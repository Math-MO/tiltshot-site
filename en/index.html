Tiltshot Support

Tiltshot turns your screenshots and screen recordings into animated 3D mockups, right on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I import a screenshot or a video?

Two ways:

  • From any app: tap Share on a screenshot or screen recording (in Photos, for example), then choose Tiltshot.
  • From Tiltshot: import from Photos or Files, or tap "Latest Screenshot" to pick up your most recent screenshot.

Tiltshot recognizes the device model from the image size and picks the matching frame. You can change it at any time.

How do I export my mockup?

Start the export from the editor and choose a format: PNG or JPEG image, MP4 video, or animated GIF. You can then save the result to Photos or Files, or share it directly.

The free version exports up to 1080p and 30 frames per second, with a "Made with Tiltshot" watermark. Tiltshot Pro unlocks 4K, 60 frames per second, transparent video and watermark-free exports.

To save to Photos, Tiltshot only asks for "add only" permission.

How do I blur sensitive information?

Use the sensitive info tool in the editor: Tiltshot finds email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, links, IBANs and card numbers on the screenshot, then blurs or pixelates them. You can also add an area by hand, or remove one.

Detection can run automatically on every import: turn it on in the app's settings. It runs entirely on the iPhone, and the original image is kept.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you already bought Tiltshot Pro (subscription or lifetime purchase), on another device or before reinstalling, tap "Restore Purchases" in the app. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Use the subscription button in the app, or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

A subscription renews automatically unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are my screenshots sent anywhere?

No. Your screenshots, videos and projects stay on your device. Tiltshot has no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
