Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Tiltshot turns your screenshots and screen recordings into 3D mockups. The app is built so that your images stay on your iPhone.

In short

  • The publisher of Tiltshot collects no data and receives no data from you.
  • Your screenshots, videos and projects are processed and stored on your device only.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple.

On-device processing

  • Your media: the screenshots and videos you import (from Photos, Files or the share sheet) are copied into the app's folder on your device. They are never sent to a server: Tiltshot has no server.
  • Your projects and settings: stored on the device, in the app's folder and in the folder shared between the app and its share extension.
  • Text recognition and sensitive info detection: reading the text in your screenshots (OCR) and spotting email addresses, phone numbers, IBANs, card numbers and the like happen on the iPhone, with Apple's frameworks. Nothing leaves the device.

Photos access

  • Read: only when you tap "Latest Screenshot", to read your most recent screenshot. Tiltshot does not browse your library.
  • Add: to save an exported mockup to Photos, with "add only" permission.

Purchases (Tiltshot Pro)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase go through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). The publisher receives neither your identity nor your payment details: the app only checks, on the device, whether a Pro purchase is active. Payment data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Unsplash

Tiltshot can offer background photos from Unsplash. This search is turned off as long as no Unsplash access key is configured in the app; in that case, no request is made to Unsplash.

If the feature is turned on and you run a search, your search query is sent to Unsplash (unsplash.com), along with the technical details of any web request (IP address, device type). When you pick a photo, it is downloaded and Unsplash is notified, as its rules require. This data is covered by Unsplash's privacy policy.

Ads and tracking

Tiltshot shows no ads, uses no analytics and does not track you across apps or websites. No data is sold, rented or shared.

Retention

Your files stay on the device for as long as you keep them: deleting a project in the gallery deletes its files, and deleting the app deletes everything. Temporary export files are removed automatically.

Children

Tiltshot collects no personal data from any user, whatever their age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address, with its update date.
